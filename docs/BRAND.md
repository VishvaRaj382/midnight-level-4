## Brand Identity Brief

- **Project Name**: AIShield
- **Tagline**: *Confidential Zero-Knowledge AI Identity on Midnight Network*
- **Description**: AIShield is a privacy-first identity and API gateway for artificial intelligence models. Utilizing Midnight Network's Compact ZK-SNARK smart contracts, it verifies user credentials and access rights locally while maintaining complete confidentiality on the public ledger.
